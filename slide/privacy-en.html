SlideGame Privacy Policy

Nocturne Games · Published November 15, 2026 · Effective November 22, 2026 · 한국어

Nocturne Games ("we", "the developer") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile puzzle game SlideGame ("the game"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the game and the choices you have.

  • No account or login
  • No developer servers
  • Google Play version: ads by Google AdMob

1. Overview

You can play the game without creating an account or logging in. We do not run any servers, and we do not directly collect or store your personal information.

The version of the game downloaded from Google Play (the "Google Play version") uses Google AdMob, Google's advertising service, to show rewarded ads. Google processes the information listed in Section 2.2 for this purpose. The One Store, Toss (Apps in Toss) and web versions do not include Google AdMob.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored only on your device (all versions)
To keep your progress, the following is stored only on your device (or in your browser's storage when played in a browser). It is never sent to us.

  • Level progress and best move counts
  • Number of hearts and heart refill time (versions with hearts only)
  • Music and sound effect settings
  • Your ad consent choice (Google Play version only)

2.2 Information collected automatically by the ad service (Google Play version only)
When ads are requested and shown, Google AdMob automatically collects:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, network connection status
  • App information: package name, app version
  • Ad interaction data: ad requests, impressions, clicks and completed views
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

3. How the information is used, kept and deleted

  • On-device information is used only to restore your game progress. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear your browser's site data. We keep no copy.
  • Ad service information is used to show ads, confirm that a rewarded ad was watched (to give you a heart), measure ad performance, prevent invalid clicks and fraud, and, where you have consented or not opted out, to show personalized ads. Google keeps and deletes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive aggregated statistics (such as impressions and earnings) that do not identify you.

4. Third parties

We do not sell, share or outsource the processing of your personal information. In the Google Play version, Google LLC collects the information in Section 2.2 directly through Google AdMob. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. International transfers

In the Google Play version, ad-related information is transferred to Google LLC and may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers. It is sent over the network when ads are requested or shown during play. Google applies its own safeguards to these transfers.

6. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown the first time you open the Google Play version. Personalized ads and the use of identifiers for advertising rely on your consent. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the ad privacy button in the game's settings screen. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

7. Your choices

  • Delete or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device's Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  • Change your ad consent with the ad privacy button in the game's settings (EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Delete all on-device data by uninstalling the game or clearing your browser's site data.
  • Request access to or deletion of data processed by Google through your Google Account settings or Google's privacy contact form.

Opting out does not stop you from playing the game.

8. Store platforms

  • Google Play: store information such as installs and payments is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • One Store: store information is handled under One Store's privacy policy.
  • Toss (Apps in Toss): Toss app usage and the ads shown inside the game are provided by Toss (Viva Republica) under its privacy policy. The game only reads the current time from the Toss server to calculate heart refills; this is not personal information.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help delete it.
